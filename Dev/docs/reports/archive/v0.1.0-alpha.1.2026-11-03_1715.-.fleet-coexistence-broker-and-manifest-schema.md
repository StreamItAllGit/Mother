## 1. Executive Summary

An empirical inspection of all six child sister repositories (`Housekeeping :5178`, `ShadowLibrary :5179`, `Redroom :5180`, `Greenroom :8420`, `StreamEngine :8430`, `legacy-sister-app :8460`) revealed three critical requirements that must be integrated into **Mother (`v1.0.0-rc.1`)** so fleet discovery and inter-app operability never fail:
1. **Heterogeneous `mother.manifest.json` Field Names in Installed Production Apps (`P0`):**
   - While all 6 child apps are upgrading their `mother.manifest.json` files to Standard 17 §5.1 `camelCase` (`schemaVersion: 1`, `id`, `name`, `brandAccent`, `healthEndpoint`, `coexistenceStatusEndpoint`), existing production installs in `<PROGRAMS_ROOT>/...` currently contain `snake_case` variants (`schema_version`, `app_id`, `display_name`, `brand_accent` / `brand_color`, and nested `endpoints: { health, status, scheduler_status, scheduler_defer }`).
   - If `Mother.Core\Discovery\AppDiscoveryService.cs` only parses strict `camelCase`, it will fail to discover or health-poll any sister app prior to its next upgrade!
2. **Missing Fleet Coexistence & Settings Conflict Aggregator (`FleetCoexistenceArbitrator.cs`, `P1`):**
   - Under the 4-Pillar Coexistence Contract ([Standard 17 §6.4](file:///<WORKSPACE_ROOT>/<DEV_BIBLE>/17_SISTER_COMPATIBILITY_AND_FEATURE_PARITY.md)), each sister app exposes `GET /api/v1/coexistence/status` and `POST /api/v1/coexistence/apply-recommendations`.
   - `Mother` (`:5100`) needs a central **Fleet Coexistence & Parity Matrix** card inside `🏛️ Fleet Overview` and `🩺 Fleet System Doctor` that polls `/api/v1/coexistence/status` across all 6 apps (and reads `<Root>\.coexistence.json` when a child daemon is offline), highlights any cross-app setting conflicts in one place, and provides a 1-click **`Align Fleet Settings`** action.
3. **Disk `*.hklock` Sentinel Visibility in `FleetScheduleTrafficCop.cs` (`P1`):**
   - `FleetScheduleTrafficCop.cs` currently arbitrates drive contention based solely on `/api/v1/scheduler/status` (`targetDrives`). If an unscheduled manual job in `ShadowLibrary`, `Housekeeping`, `Redroom`, `legacy-sister-app`, or `StreamEngine` is actively holding a `<Dir>\.<app>_<pid>.hklock` sentinel on a target drive, the Traffic Cop should detect that active lock and defer competing scheduled tasks on the same drive.

## 5. Constraints & Architectural Recommendations for `@pland`

1. **Dual-Schema Tolerant `AppDiscoveryService.cs` (`<400 LOC`):**
   - Normalize `id` (`id ?? app_id`), `name` (`name ?? display_name`), `brandAccent` (`brandAccent ?? brand_accent ?? brand_color`), and endpoint URLs (checking top-level `camelCase` `*Endpoint` first, then `snake_case` `*_endpoint`, then nested `endpoints.*`, and falling back to Standard 17 `/api/v1/*` defaults on `port`).
2. **Add `FleetCoexistenceArbitrator.cs` (`<400 LOC`) & UI Matrix in `🏛️ Fleet Overview`:**
   - Poll `GET /api/v1/coexistence/status` across running sister apps (and inspect `.coexistence.json` on shared roots when a peer is stopped).
   - Expose `GET /api/v1/coexistence/status` (fleet aggregate) and `POST /api/v1/coexistence/apply-recommendations` (forwarding preset alignment to the target child app or updating `.coexistence.json`).
   - Add a **Fleet Coexistence & Shared Storage Matrix** panel in `🏛️ Fleet Overview` with conflict warning banners, 1-click preset buttons, and a fleet-wide **`Auto-Align Shared Settings Across Fleet`** toggle.
3. **Enhance `FleetScheduleTrafficCop.cs` with `*.hklock` & Coexistence Awareness:**
   - Check both `/api/v1/scheduler/status` (`activeJobs > 0` / `isRunning`) and active `*.hklock` sentinels on `managedRoots` before allowing a second disk-intensive scheduled job to start on the same physical drive.
