   - Mother's logger must also enforce Standard 15 §5.1 structured log format (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.fffZ [v<Version>] [PID:X/T:Y] [LEVEL] [slice] message`) and Standard 12 §3.2 log rotation (`10MB` max size, `5` generations, `30-day` pruning).

3. **Enforce Standard 12 Clean Release Root (`app\` + `scripts\`) in `Builds\v1.0.0-rc.1\` & `Mother.iss` (`Task 4.2`, `Task 5.1`, `Task 5.2`):**
