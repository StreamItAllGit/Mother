## 1. Executive Summary
Following the fleet-wide `/grill-me` architecture review across all seven projects (**Housekeeping**, **ShadowLibrary**, **Greenroom**, **Redroom**, **legacy-sister-app**, **StreamEngine**, and **Mother**), several critical **fleet-wide responsibilities were explicitly moved out of the individual sister apps and centralized into `Mother` (`v1.0`)**, and the packaging rollout schedule was locked in (`Mother v1.0` developed standalone first, then packaged/co-installed with all sister apps starting in **`v1.1`**).

This report documents the architectural updates that `@pland` must incorporate into [`Dev\docs\plans\v1.0.0-rc.1.2026-11-02.md`](file:///<WORKSPACE_ROOT>/Mother/Dev/docs/plans/v1.0.0-rc.1.2026-11-02.md) so **Mother** serves as the true fleet supervisor and orchestrator:
1. **Fleet-Wide Post-Run Power Coordinator (`StayAwake / Sleep / Shutdown` Moved to `Mother v1.0`):** Individual sister apps will no longer expose standalone Sleep/Shutdown triggers (which risked putting the PC to sleep while another sister app was mid-write on a 50 GB archive or ISO conversion). **Mother `v1.0`** owns the global **Post-Run Power Action (`StayAwake / Sleep / Shutdown`)** selector, monitoring the aggregate status of all registered sister apps and only triggering OS Sleep/Shutdown after **every** active and queued job across the entire fleet reaches `Idle` (with a 60-second cancellable countdown toast).
2. **Centralized Master Watchdog (`\Mother_Watchdog` Moved to `Mother v1.0`):** Individual sister apps (`Housekeeping`, `Greenroom`) are retiring their per-app Windows Task Scheduler watchdogs. **Mother `v1.0`** owns the single **`\Mother_Watchdog`** Windows Task Scheduler entry and continuously supervises, health-polls, and restarts all registered child sister daemons (`--managed-by-mother`).
3. **Fleet Schedule Traffic Cop / Disk I/O Mutex Arbitration:** While each sister app maintains its own `Schedules` (`Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Interval` + `RunIfMissed`) so it can operate standalone, **Mother `v1.0`** acts as the **Global Schedule Traffic Cop**—polling `/api/scheduler/status` and issuing `/api/scheduler/defer` locks so two disk-intensive sister apps (e.g., `Housekeeping` + `ShadowLibrary` or `Redroom`) never thrash the same physical drive simultaneously at 3:00 AM.
4. **Orthogonal Navigation Harmony (Outer Horizontal App Tabs + Inner Left Sidebar Rails):** Because all 6 sister apps are standardizing on **Greenroom's Left Sidebar Rail (`220px`/`56px`) + Slim Top Action Bar (`48px`)**, **Mother `v1.0`** owns the **Top Horizontal Axis** (`Alt+1..6` outer app tab strip + Fleet Overview tab + Global Post-Run Power selector + `🛑 Fleet Stop All`) with zero "double horizontal tab bar" visual collision.
5. **WinForms Single-Surface Dark GUI Installer (`ShadowLibrary rc.10-hf.2` Standard) & `v1.1` Co-Packaging Gate:** Replace Inno Setup in the `Mother` plan with the **C# WinForms Single-Surface Dark GUI Setup & Uninstall Wizard** (`InstallerWizardForm.cs`, `650×434` -> `650×544`) + **Clean Release Root** (`Mother.exe` at root, runtime in `app\`, scripts in `scripts\` and `scripts\interactive\`). Co-installation inside the 6 sister-app installers is gated for **`v1.1`** once `Mother v1.0` is built and verified.
